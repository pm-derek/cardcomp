Recognition test

Experiment, stored only in this extension, kept 30 days. Turn it on in the CardComp panel ("auto off" under the lot strip). A lot where you tapped a bid or opened a card is scored from that card automatically. Lots you did not touch are listed first under "to review": right = auto was correct, listed, not picked = auto wrong but the card was in the list, wrong = the card was not in the list, no card = nothing on screen.
